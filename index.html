HOW TO INTERPRET

Bass-heavy / boomy: comparatively strong low-band energy. Muffled: weak presence region relative to body (context matters). Bright / sharp: stronger upper-band energy. Harsh: can involve excessive upper energy, distortion, clipping or noise. Use listening tests alongside the display; one spectrum is not enough to prescribe GM950 settings.
