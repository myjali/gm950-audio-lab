V1.0 ANALYSIS GUIDE
Best voice balance (visual guide): concentrate on intelligible speech energy across roughly 300 Hz–3.4 kHz, with no strong bass rumble, excessive hiss/harshness, or obvious clipping. There is no universal “ideal” spectrum for every speaker or voice.

Limits: the phone microphone, room acoustics, speaker, AGC, noise reduction and distance affect the displayed spectrum. Treat it as a repeatable comparison tool, not a calibrated audio test instrument.
